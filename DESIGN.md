# Design

Murmur has almost no chrome — a menu bar item and a small settings panel — so the
design work is about restraint. The app should feel like part of macOS, not like
a thing bolted onto it.

## Archetype: **Soft product**

Generous radii, layered translucency, soft ambient shadow. Chosen because Murmur
lives in the menu bar next to system affordances, and because the sibling fleet
has not used it: `ableton-ai` is Console, `chordic` and `termite` are Poster,
`thegrid` is Terminal, `sidney` is Blueprint, `finscreen` is Brutalist.

## Axis picks

| Axis | Pick | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Layout** | Centred single column in a ~340px popover, hung off the menu bar item | There is no navigation to hold — one screen, a few controls. Every recent sibling docks a rail or tiles a grid; none of them is a popover. |
| **Type scale** | Near-flat, 1.2 | A settings panel with a dramatic scale looks like a landing page. Hierarchy comes from Bricolage's weight and width axes instead. |
| **Surface** | Translucent-blurred over the desktop, matching `NSVisualEffectView` | The one place where copying the platform is correct — a menu bar popover that is opaque reads as foreign. |
| **Radius** | 12px on panels, pill on the toggle and primary button | Soft throughout. No hard edges anywhere; nothing here represents time or a grid, so there is nothing to square off. |
| **Accent** | **Gradient pair** — iris `#3B2E8F` → coral `#E8615F`, through magenta `#8A3F9B` | Unused across the fleet: siblings ran duotone (violet/lime, mint/violet, blue/yellow), a phosphor ramp, and per-category. The gradient carries the icon and the recording state; a flat accent would have made the waveform generic. |
| **Motion signature** | **Blur-in** — content arrives at `blur(8px)` and resolves over 320ms | Unused by siblings (sweep, stagger-cascade, wipe-reveal, draw-in, snap-slide, rise-and-fade, press-depress, scale-in-bloom, rail-slide). It is also the right metaphor: speech resolving into text is exactly what the app does. |
| **Ground texture** | Fine noise over the blurred material | Stops the translucency banding on a flat desktop wallpaper. |

Mono slot: **Spline Sans Mono**, for the latency readout and the transcript preview.

## Icon

`Assets/icon.svg` → `Assets/Murmur.icns`, regenerated by `Scripts/bundle.sh`.

A squircle carrying the iris→coral gradient, with five white pill bars. The bar
heights are deliberately asymmetric (176 / 352 / 512 / 304 / 128) so it reads as
speech rather than as a symmetrical level meter, which is the shape every other
audio app uses. Verified legible at 16px.
